Compositional Multimodal Adapter

Action-conditioned multi-stream world model on a frozen DynamiCrafter video prior  ·  build → train → per-component denoising

One frozen video diffusion model. A small trainable adapter that makes it also predict proprioception and tactile/depth — and lets those extra senses improve the video prediction in return. Nothing in the base is touched.

frozen = pretrained, no gradient trainable = the adapter (12.8M params) video proprio tactile action

1 How we build it

The base predicts the future video. Everything else is the adapter. Two ideas wire the extra modalities to the video without changing the base:

Compositional = one adapter per modality. Each modality has its own AVID adapter emitting its own video adjustment Δ_m; a single learned mask m ∈ ℝn+2 blends them with the base and the action adapter. The mask is the contribution — its softmax weights show exactly how much each modality is used.

video ← m  (modalities help the video): each noised modality → a few tokens, injected into only its own adapter's cross-attention context. No modality attends to another (that edge is reserved for the fused variant).

m ← video  (video helps the modalities): the frozen base's video prediction is pooled into a conditioning vector fed to each modality head, so each modality is denoised with knowledge of the video.

2 How we train the adapter

Frozen no gradient

• DynamiCrafter UNet (the video prior)
• First-stage VAE (video ↔ latent)
• OpenCLIP image/text encoders (context)

Trainable 35.2M params

• AVID adapter per stream (action + 1 per modality ≈ 11M each)
• LearnedMaskFusion mask m ∈ ℝn+2 (the contribution)
• ModalityEncoder per modality (video←m)
• VideoReadout (m←video)
• ModalityHead per modality
• action conditioning encoder

The objective — one summed, weighted denoising loss

Each output stream is its own denoising problem; the adapter is trained on the weighted sum of the per-stream losses (the UWM scheme).

L = Σm wm · Lmdenoise   = 1.0·Lvideo + 0.5·Lproprio + 0.2·Ltactile
Why joint training matters. Every part learns because all losses flow at once: each modality's encoder + adapter (video←m) gets gradient through its context tokens (from Lvideo via the mask), the m←video readout gets gradient through the modality heads (from Lproprio/Ltactile), and the learned mask m gets gradient directly (it decides how much each Δ counts). Training the modality streams stops them collapsing to noise — which is what makes them informative for the video correction.

Independent per-modality timesteps (UWM)

Crucially, the streams are not noised at a shared t. Each draws its own tm ~ U(0,T). At inference this single trained model gives — for free — policy (action | states), forward/inverse dynamics, and pure video prediction, by choosing which streams start from noise.

3 How each component trains through denoising

Every stream — video, proprio, tactile — is supervised by the same diffusion recipe, just at its own timestep. One forward pass predicts all streams; one backward updates the whole adapter.

for each stream m, per step:
  tm ~ U(0,T) ,   ε ~ 𝒩(0,I)
  xtm = √ᾱtm · x0m + √(1−ᾱtm) · ε   # q_sample: noise the clean target
  targetm = v = √ᾱtm·ε − √(1−ᾱtm)·x0m   # velocity parameterisation
  Lm = ‖ predm − targetm ‖²

The difference that matters: the video stream is a correction on a frozen prior (m·ε_pre + … with the base-biased mask) — the base already knows how to denoise video, the adapters only nudge it. The modality streams have no prior; their heads predict the whole denoising target from scratch. Both are trained by the identical MSE-on-velocity loss, summed.

Status. The compositional path runs end-to-end on the real DynamiCrafter UNet (contract tests + a random-weight / synthetic-batch smoke run, 35.2M trainable params = 3 AVID adapters + mask + encoders + readout + heads). It is not a training result yet — a real run needs the pretrained checkpoint + a MetaWorld loader emitting the modality streams. The three "works" criteria (modalities predicted · video improves · modalities demonstrably used) are untested.
