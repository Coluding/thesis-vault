## 1. The rubric (verbatim)

Ten items in three categories — five Research, three Thesis, two Attitude.
Each is scored 1–10; **scores 1–5 collapse
to a single failing description** in every item, which means the whole
discriminating range is **6–10**. Read that structurally: the rubric does
not distinguish degrees of failure, only degrees of quality above
competence.

## 2. The structural read — what this rubric rewards

Three observations that should drive every writing decision:

**(a) Not one of the ten items scores the *outcome* of the research.** No rubric line
says "the method worked". *Originality* scores the question and framing;
*Technical skills* and *Experimental evaluation* score the rigour;
*Knowledge* and *Reflection* score the understanding. A thesis whose
headline is "we measured why this class of method fails, and located the
boundary" can score at the top of every item. **This is the single most
important fact about this document's prospects** — the outcome the user
worries about is not what is being graded.

**(b) Six of the ten items are pure writing craft.** *Knowledge*,
*Reflection*, *Literature*, *Organization*, *Writing*, and to a large
extent *Experimental evaluation* are scored on **what the document says
about the work**, not on the work. The experiments are largely done; the
grade is mostly still unwritten. Effort spent on prose is not overhead.

**(c) The rubric's own language names the upgrade path.** Compare the 8 and
9 rows and the delta is always *generalisation beyond this thesis*:

| Item | 8 → 9 delta, in the rubric's words |
|---|---|
| Technical skills | "commonly used checks" → "**thorough** checks and **advanced and original** analyses" |
| Experimental evaluation | "considers error and uncertainty quantitatively" → "**exactly tailored to answering the research questions**" + near-flawless execution |
| Knowledge | "add new knowledge to the study domain" → "**place the field under a new light**" |
| Reflection | "understands pros and cons" → "**other researchers in the field can be helped**" |
| Literature | "nicely synthesized… complete" → "clear, complete, **relevant**" |
| Writing | "interesting lessons learned" → "**thought-provoking** … **novel analyses that further enhance the thesis**" |

Four of six say the same thing: **write the transferable lesson, not just
the local finding.** For this thesis that lesson is concrete and already
measured — *the standard readouts (loss, gate, FID, sample quality) are
blind to whether a conditioned model uses its conditioning; here is a
probe suite that is not.* Every rubric item above has that sentence as its
9-row.

### 3.1 Originality — *strong, and stronger than the "negative results" worry suggests*

**Carried by.** The framing decided 2026-08-02 — *an empirical study of
when and why adapter-based adaptation works*, not a systems build — is a
**fresh perspective on an existing framework** (rubric 8's exact words:
"the problem is tackled from a fresh perspective"). The specific
mechanistic claim is the originality:

> Adapters can make a video foundation model action-conditioned, **but only
> when the conditioning enters through per-frame modulation of normalised
> activations**. The same action supplied as cross-attention tokens
> survives the attention and drowns at the residual add — because AdaLN
> multiplies a *normalised* activation and is scale-free by construction,
> while cross-attention adds into an unnormalised residual stream.

That is architecture-level, mechanism-level, and transfers to any
conditioning problem on a DiT. It is the most publishable sentence in the
vault ([[thesis-storyline]] §REFRAME, sourced to the clean-room A/B).

**What holds it below 9.** Nothing here *steers the sub-field*; it
sharpens a design rule inside an existing paradigm (adapter-based world
models, post-AVID). The honest band is **7–8**, landing on 8 if the
pathway claim is the thesis's headline and is stated as a general design
rule rather than a Wan implementation detail.

**Cheapest upgrade.** Frame the pathway finding as a **design principle
with a stated scope of validity** ("conditioning must enter scale-free
relative to the residual stream; we verify this on two architectures and
two injection sites") rather than as a bug report about one adapter. Same
evidence, materially different originality read.

**⚠ The risk to manage.** Originality collapses toward 6 ("a variation of
existing work, applying an existing approach to another problem") if the
thesis reads as *"we applied AVID to Wan"*. The defence is the reframe
plus [[../../10_now/positioning]]'s corrected D2 delta — AVID is the
starting point, the pathway/economics analysis is the delta. **This must
be explicit in §1.4, not implied.**

### 3.2 Technical skills — *the strongest item; rubric-9 language already fits*

**Carried by.** Rubric 9 asks for "thorough checks" and "advanced and
original analyses". The vault has, all sourced:

- **Purpose-built probe suite** — `effect_rel`, null control,
  `--emb-scale` sweep, Jacobian sensitivity, 23-depth propagation trace,
  rollout-action-swap, and the structure triad (steering cosine, temporal
  alignment, spatial concentration). Original instruments, not standard
  ones.
- **Verified nulls, not assumed** — `base_null_violation` exactly 0
  throughout; frozen-base invariance checked before every run.
- **Matched controls on every headline** — matched adapter contribution
  (0.114 vs 0.111) and matched mask (0.953) in the clean-room A/B; a
  measured noise floor (0.000245) for the DC parity arms.
- **Statistics** — Welch t-tests (t=3.30 → 7.3 → 10.5 across depths),
  bootstrap CIs reported as disjoint intervals (0.302 [0.251,0.356] vs
  0.034 [0.026,0.042]).
- **Root-causing to the tensor level** — the DC pedestal (embedding grows
  106×, 99.7% constant, 14× the time embedding) and the Wan drowning
  (xattn RMS ~0.01 against a stream of 1.8–3.0, drel 0.44→0.0085 across
  one addition).

Band: **8–9**.

**Cheapest upgrade.** Two things stand between this and a clean 9:
1. **Seed variance.** Every headline is single-seed; the CIs are over
   evaluation batches, which is a different source of uncertainty. Two
   extra seeds on the *one* headline A/B converts "advanced analysis" into
   "thorough checks" in the rubric's sense. Cheap, high leverage.
2. **Write the probe suite as an instrument, with validation.** A short
   subsection: what each probe measures, its null, its chance level, and
   the case where it was *wrong* (the `effect_rel` gain confound and how
   the temporal-control probe resolved it). Validating your own instrument
   is exactly "advanced and original analyses on the data".

### 3.3 Experimental evaluation — *8 secured, 9 within reach*

**Carried by.** Rubric 8: "judge the setup of an existing experiment and
include modifications if needed; considers sources of error and
uncertainty quantitatively." The vault does this repeatedly and visibly:

- **Hypothesis-first ablation design** ([[thesis-storyline]] §7) — the
  candidate explanations for the collapse are enumerated, then the axis
  that discriminates each. This is the difference between science and
  flailing, and the rubric grades it directly.
- **Confound identification and resolution.** The gain-vs-information
  confound was named, then *resolved* by a temporal-control probe
  (diagonal concentration 0.390 vs 0.199 against chance 0.200; the pooled
  arm's per-frame response rows are bit-identical).
- **Pre-registered thresholds** (the NOBASE erosion prediction was
  registered before the run and marked ✓).
- **Invalidations found and acted on** — the SkyReels cell retracted
  (`dataset_size=76`, a silent 98.5% episode drop, plus in-sample eval);
  the in-sample RT-1/OpenVid evaluations quarantined; the gate-bug-
  confounded 68× superseded by a clean measurement.

**What holds it below 9.** Rubric 9 requires execution to be "nearly
flawless". It was not — and the flaws are documented in your own vault:
the silent window filter, the missing held-out split, the frozen-gate bug,
the cross-base shortcut A/B that varies consistency target *and* depth
simultaneously. Band: **7–8**, landing on 8 because every one of those was
**self-detected and corrected**, which is what "judge the setup … and
include modifications if needed" describes.

**Cheapest upgrade — and this is the most important single item in this
document.** Turn the execution failures into an explicit *methods
integrity* subsection rather than letting the committee find them. Each
one, in the five-part negative-result shape
([[thesis-style-guide]] §5): what was wrong, how it was detected, what it
invalidated, what was re-run. A reader who sees you found your own
in-sample evaluation stops looking for the one you missed. **Hiding these
is the highest-risk option available; foregrounding them is close to free
and moves both this item and Reflection.**

**⚠ The one unresolved threat.** The cross-base shortcut comparison
(flow vs diffusion) is flagged CONFOUNDED — the arms differ in consistency
target *and* depth. The within-arm results stand. The draft must state the
confound in the same paragraph as the 9× number, or the number will be
attacked and the whole D3 claim with it.

### 3.4 Knowledge of study domain — *8 on evidence, currently under-documented*

**Carried by.** Rubric 8 is "on top of subjects discussed; able to add new
knowledge". The curvature analysis is the clearest case: the sagitta
argument (the arithmetic mean is the Euclidean centroid, the manifold
wants the Riemannian one; the true field is not a fixed point of the
averaging rule, so the bias compounds up the doubling tower) is a
**derivation**, and it is corroborated numerically at zero model error
(averaged target 5.1% / 16.1% / 24.1% off at s = 1/4, 1/2, 3/4; endpoint
target 0.000000). Theory and measurement agreeing is the strongest form
this item takes. The AVID correction — recognising that action
conditioning on a frozen base is AVID's contribution and repositioning
accordingly — is also a *Knowledge* signal, and an expensive error avoided.

Band: **8**, with **9** reachable ("place the field under a new light")
*only* via §2(c): generalise the pathway + economics findings into a claim
about conditioning-on-frozen-priors as a class.

**⚠ Restriction.** Two of the domain's own traps are live here
(CLAUDE.md Part 12): the *diffusion vs flow* prediction-type distinction,
and *shortcut* vs generic consistency distillation. A single sloppy
sentence — "shortcut modelling fails on diffusion" — inverts this item
from a strength to a demonstrated misunderstanding, because it is *false*
and a committee member will know it. See [[thesis-style-guide]] §4.

### 3.5 Reflection — *the largest gap between what exists and what is written*

**Carried by.** The raw material is unusually strong and almost entirely
unwritten. The vault contains, in its own history: a headline claim
retracted with its cause identified; an inference superseded while its
measurement was preserved (the 07-29 "blindness is a data/OOD problem"
reading, killed by the 07-30 AVID-on-our-data control); a level claim
retired when the gap failed to converge (DC arm E's 3.5× → 2.5×, keeping
only the ~6× acceleration); a primary metric identified as confounded *by
its own author*, then discriminated by a purpose-built control.

Rubric 9 is "reflect and learn from their own work **in such a way that
other researchers in the field can be helped**." That is a description of
the methodological finding, stated as a warning:

> Loss, gate statistics, FID and sample quality are **all** blind to
> whether a conditioned model uses its conditioning. A model can improve
> on every standard perceptual metric — Wan × ACWM beats its frozen base
> 6/6, FVD −64% — while all three structure probes sit at chance. Anyone
> training a conditioned generative model on standard readouts alone
> cannot tell these apart.

Band: **6–7 as currently drafted** (the reflection lives in vault notes,
not in `70_Thesis/draft/`); **9 is achievable** and is the single best
return on writing effort in the whole document.

**Cheapest upgrade.** A dedicated chapter section — not a Discussion
paragraph — titled for what it is: the standard metrics are blind, here is
what we used instead, here is what it cost us to find out, here is the
false trail we followed and why. Include the hypotheses that *died*
(σ-mismatch, "our data is too hard", "the DiT capacity is the problem").
Reporting dead hypotheses is what makes the surviving one credible.

### 3.6 Use of literature — *the weakest item, and the most mechanically fixable*

**Carried by.** The paper notes that exist are genuinely synthesised, not
summarised — the AVID note carries a Correction section that repositioned

the research at hand").

**The gap.** `30_Knowledge/related-work/` holds **13 notes**. The rubric's
8 and 9 rows both hinge on the word **complete**, and the vault names its
own holes:

- **`controlnet.md` and `lora.md` do not exist** — and

- **World-model literature is thin** relative to a thesis whose title is

- **Few-step sampling** needs to be complete enough that the D3 comparison

Band: **6–7 today** ("literature study is not complete" is the literal
6-row). This is the one item where the score is currently set by a
*missing file count*, not by judgement.

**Cheapest upgrade.** This is bounded, mechanical work with a known list,
and it is the highest-certainty point gain available: write the missing
notes, then structure Ch2 by *tension in the field* rather than by paper
([[thesis-style-guide]] §6). Also cite the **negative space** explicitly —
the flow-model/temporal-VAE search that came back empty is a finding when
its scope is stated, and an omission when it is silent.

## 4. The three things that would cost the most

Ranked by expected damage, most damaging first.

**1. Writing "the approach does not work on Wan".** It contradicts the
vault's own measurements — Wan × ACWM beats the frozen base 6/6 with
FVD −64%, and the clean-room per-frame arm beats the AVID/DynamiCrafter
reference at matched depth. It also discards the thesis's most original
claim (the pathway result) in exchange for a weaker one. The correct
statement is:

> The pathway decides. Per-frame modulation of normalised activations
> works; cross-attention tokens do not, at any scale or alignment we
> tested. **Rollout-level control is not demonstrated** — the rollout-swap
> probe is null on Wan and has not been run on DC.

The second sentence is the honest ceiling and belongs in the abstract. It
is a *limit*, not a failure — and it is exactly the kind of limit that
scores well under *Reflection*.

⚠ **Corrected 2026-08-03:** an earlier version of this section said
"on no cell do we demonstrate action control". That understates the DC
cell, whose structure triad clears chance on all three axes on held-out
data. State the ceiling **per cell** — see [[rubric/_index]].

**2. Drafting from a superseded vault layer.** [[writing-plan-2026-08]],
`index.md`'s chapter numbering, and the positioning anti-positioning
bullet all predate the 08-02 reframe. Reconcile before drafting.
Cost of doing it now: about an hour. Cost of doing it after two chapters:
those two chapters.

**3. Letting `effect_rel` carry claims it cannot support.** It is monotone
in action-pathway gain — the vault established this itself. It was
discriminated *for the clean-room A/B* by the temporal-control probe; it is
**not** discriminated for the other cells. Two mitigations, both already
identified in [[thesis-storyline]]:
- Adopt **AVID's Action Error Ratio** (their §4.2) as a second, external,
  published readout, so the D2 table is comparable to their baselines
  rather than to an instrument of our own construction.
- Where only `effect_rel` is available, write at rung 3
  ([[thesis-style-guide]] §2) and name the alternative explanation in the
  same paragraph.
