Diffusion Forcing

a visual explainer · world-model conditioning via per-frame noise levels
One line: standard diffusion gives every frame the same noise level; diffusion forcing lets every frame have its own. Once a frame's noise level is independent, it becomes a conditioning knob — a frame at σ = 0 is given (observed), a frame at σ = 1 is generated. Image-to-video, text-to-video and autoregressive rollout become the same model run with a different per-frame schedule — no CLIP encoder, no concat channel.

1 · The σ axis — “how forced” a frame is

Each frame carries a noise level σ ∈ [0,1]. It generalises teacher forcing (binary given/absent) into a continuous dial:

σ = 0 · forced (clean, given) partially forced (noisy hint) σ = 1 · free (generate)

3 · Watch image-to-video denoise

The observation frame is pinned clean the whole time; the future frames start as noise and denoise down — guided, through attention, by that clean frame. This is exactly our sampler loop (re-clamp the observation each step).

Same trained weights — you just choose the per-frame σ trajectory at sampling time:

Image → Video (ours)frame 0 pinned σ=0; rest anneal 1 → 0
Autoregressive / ∞ videopast σ=0, future noisier with horizon; slide window
Inpaint / interpolateknown frames σ=0, gaps noised

5 · What we actually run (important nuance)

  • Capability vs usage. The model is built to accept a fully independent σ per frame (that's the per-token timestep). But our I2V uses a two-level schedule: σ = [0, s, s, s, …] — observation at 0, all future frames sharing one annealing s. Not all-different.
  • Masked loss. Observed frames (σ=0) get no gradient — their velocity target references an unobservable noise draw. We supervise only the predicted frames.
  • Re-clamp every step. The scheduler nudges all frames, so the observation is rewritten to its clean latent after each step or it drifts.
  • Why it fixes our world model. Old T2V setup: hallucinate the whole scene from noise + a 4-dim action (ill-posed → washed-out eval). Now: the model is given the present and only predicts the consequences of the actions — well-posed.
  • All-different earns its keep for long rollout (the ramp preset): cleaner near “now”, noisier far ahead → uncertainty grows with horizon; slide the window to generate indefinitely.
