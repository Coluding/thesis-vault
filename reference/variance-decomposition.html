The three identities

1 · L2 recovers the conditional mean

E[(Y − f(X))²] = E[Var[Y|X]] + E[(E[Y|X] − f(X))²] argminf ⇒ f(X) = E[Y|X]

Squared error cannot learn anything else. The residual E[Var[Y|X]] is a floor.

2 · Law of total variance (the split)

Var[Y|X] = EA[Var[Y|X,A]] + VarA(E[Y|X,A]) irreducible action-explained

3 · The ceiling

best blind MSE − best action-aware MSE = E[VarA(E[Y|X,A])]

The total loss reduction available from actions, for any architecture. A property of data + representation, not of the model.

The normalisation is the whole trick. Raw losses are not comparable across backbones — different latent spaces (4-ch SD-VAE vs 48-ch Wan-VAE), different target scalings. Divide by Var[target] and they are:
R² = 1 − E[(Y − f)²] / Var[Y] how much X determines Y ΔR²act = action_loss_gap / Var[Y] how much A adds
Both dimensionless. This is the only way to put DC and Wan on one axis.

Traps

trapwhy it bites
Comparing raw eval_loss across backbonesdifferent latent dims and target scalings; DC 0.026 vs Wan 0.13 says nothing on its own
Reading a low ceiling as "the adapter failed"the bound is pre-architectural; every injection site hits the same wall
Assuming blinding to x_t lowers the ceilingit raises the action-explained share — explaining-away is removed
Averaging over tambiguity varies hugely with noise level; always report per-σ
Treating it as a diffusion-only resultit is a property of L2; flow matching inherits it identically

Explaining away, in one line

VarA(E[Y|A]) ≥ VarA(E[Y|X,A])

Conditioning on X can only shrink what A is left to explain. Hence the action-only arm has the higher action ceiling and the worse absolute loss.
