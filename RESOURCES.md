# Resources

## Primary

- **Bishop, _Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning_ (2006), §1.5.5** — derives
  the decomposition of expected squared loss into model error + intrinsic noise,
  and `E[t|x]` as the minimiser. The cleanest print statement of Lesson 1 §1.
  [Free PDF (Microsoft Research)](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/uploads/prod/2006/01/Bishop-Pattern-Recognition-and-Machine-Learning-2006.pdf)
  · §3.2 for the bias–variance version.
- **Bishop §1.5.5 → §3.2** is also the bridge to the bias–variance decomposition,
  which is the same algebra applied to the model-error term rather than the noise term.
